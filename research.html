Research

Currently, I am doing my PhD student in Mathematics under the guidance of Prof. Neela Nataraj (IIT Bombay) and Prof. Ricardo Ruiz Baier (Monash University). My research interests lie broadly in the areas of partial differential equations (PDEs), numerical analysis, and finite element methods (FEM).

My doctoral research focuses on the theoretical and numerical analysis of coupled multiphysics problems, with particular emphasis on developing, analysing, and implementing reliable numerical methods for complex systems governed by coupled partial differential equations.

Current Research Topics

Poisson–Fermi Interface Problems

Mathematical modelling and numerical analysis of Poisson–Fermi interface problems, with an emphasis on the mathematical formulation, finite element approximation, and theoretical properties of the resulting numerical schemes.

Fluid–Structure Interaction

Mathematical and computational investigation of coupled fluid and structural systems, focusing on numerical approximation, stability, and convergence of finite element methods.

Coupled Multiphysics Problems

Development and analysis of numerical frameworks for systems involving interacting physical processes and coupled partial differential equations.

Research Methodology

A central aspect of my research is the study of the mathematical properties of numerical schemes, including their well-posedness, stability, convergence, and error estimates. I am particularly interested in finite element discretisations and their theoretical foundations.

Research Objectives

Through my research, I aim to contribute to the development of mathematically rigorous and computationally efficient numerical methods for solving challenging coupled multiphysics problems.
