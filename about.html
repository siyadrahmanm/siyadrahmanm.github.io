About me

I am a PhD student in Mathematics at the IITB-Monash Research Academy, working under the guidance of Prof. Neela Nataraj (IIT Bombay) and Prof. Ricardo Ruiz Baier (Monash University). My fascination with mathematics dates back to my primary school years, when I first discovered the joy of exploring mathematical ideas. Over time, this early curiosity grew into a deeper enthusiasm for research, which blossomed during my second year of undergraduate studies when I was selected for the Science Academies Summer Research Fellowship Program (2020). Under the supervision of Dr. Sheetal Dharmatti (IISER Thiruvananthapuram), I worked on Calculus of Variations and Fourier Analysis. This experience offered me my first glimpse into mathematical research and encouraged me to explore the subject beyond the classroom. I later extended this work as my undergraduate project under the guidance of Dr. Sameena Kalathodi at MES Mampad College.

I completed my Bachelor’s degree in Mathematics at MES Mampad College (Autonomous), University of Calicut, followed by a Master’s degree from IISER Thiruvananthapuram. During my Master's studies, I worked on Optimal Impulse Control Problems and Viscosity Solutions under the supervision of Dr. Sheetal Dharmatti at IISER Thiruvananthapuram. This project further broadened my exposure to mathematical analysis and strengthened my interest in research. Subsequently, I undertook an online research internship under the guidance of Prof. Neela Nataraj (IIT Bombay), where I was introduced to finite element methods.

Originally from Nilambur, a picturesque town in Kerala known for its lush greenery, forests, and teak plantations, I carry a fondness for the natural beauty and tranquillity of my hometown.

My research interests lie primarily in partial differential equations, numerical analysis, and finite element methods. I am particularly interested in the interplay between mathematical theory and computational techniques in the analysis and numerical solution of differential equations.

Beyond academia, I find joy in the world of words and creativity. I enjoy reading, writing stories and poetry, exploring music and cinema, and indulging in various craftwork. There is something deeply fulfilling about creating, whether through words, art, or mathematics.
